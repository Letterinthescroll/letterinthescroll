Ashkenazi Mushroom Barley Soup

A thick, deeply savory soup of sautéed cremini and shiitake mushrooms, pearled barley, and root vegetables in a rich broth — old-world Ashkenazi comfort that improves enormously the next day.

Prep Time20 min
Cook Time1 hr
Total Time1 hr 20 min
Servings8
DifficultyEasy
Shabbat Sukkot Yom Kippur Vegan Option

Ingredients

Adjust servings:
  • 1 lb cremini mushrooms, sliced
  • 4 oz shiitake mushrooms, stems removed, sliced
  • 3/4 cup pearled barley
  • 2 large onions, diced
  • 3 carrots, peeled and diced
  • 3 celery stalks, diced
  • 8 cups beef or vegetable stock
  • 3 tbsp neutral oil
  • 1/4 cup fresh dill, chopped
  • Salt and freshly ground black pepper

Directions

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Add the mushrooms in a single layer — do not stir for the first 5 minutes. Let them sit undisturbed until a golden-brown crust forms on the bottom. Then stir and continue cooking until all liquid has evaporated, about 10 minutes total.
  2. Push the mushrooms to one side. Add the diced onions, carrots, and celery to the same pot. Sauté for 5 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the barley and pour in the stock. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 1 hour until the barley is tender and the soup has thickened naturally.
  4. Stir in the fresh dill, season generously with salt and pepper, and serve.
Chef's Tip

Never stir the mushrooms for the first 5 minutes. Letting them sit undisturbed on high heat creates a golden-brown crust that adds a deep, meaty umami to the soup.
