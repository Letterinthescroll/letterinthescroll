Green Schug (Yemenite Herbal Chili Paste)

A verdant, aromatic paste of fresh jalapeños, cilantro, parsley, and cardamom — the cooling yet fiery Yemenite condiment that is the essential counterpart to hilbe on any Yemenite Shabbat table.

Prep Time15 min
Cook Time0 min
Total Time15 min
Servings1
DifficultyEasy
Shabbat Friends Vegan Condiment

Ingredients

Adjust servings:
  • 4–5 fresh jalapeños, stemmed (seeds removed for milder version)
  • 1 large bunch fresh cilantro (leaves and tender stems)
  • 1/2 bunch fresh flat-leaf parsley
  • 4 garlic cloves
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp ground cardamom
  • 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil

Directions

  1. Roughly chop the jalapeños into large chunks.
  2. Place the cilantro, parsley, and garlic in a food processor. Pulse a few times to break down the leaves.
  3. Add the jalapeño chunks, salt, cardamom, and olive oil. Process until the mixture resembles a coarse pesto — not a smooth purée.
  4. Taste and adjust salt and heat. Transfer to a jar and cover with a thin layer of olive oil. Refrigerate for up to 10 days.
Chef's Tip

Cardamom is the secret ingredient in Yemenite green schug. It provides a floral, cooling top-note that balances the intense capsaicin heat of the jalapeños and the pungency of the garlic.
