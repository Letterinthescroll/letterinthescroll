Moroccan Chicken Pastilla (B'stilla)

An extraordinary savory pie of shredded chicken, silky egg custard, and sweetened almonds wrapped in crispy phyllo and dusted with powdered sugar and cinnamon. The ultimate Moroccan showpiece.

Prep Time1 hr
Cook Time1 hr 35 min
Total Time2 hrs 35 min
Servings8
DifficultyAdvanced
Special Guests Rosh Hashanah

Ingredients

Adjust servings:
  • 1 kg (2.2 lbs) bone-in, skin-on chicken thighs
  • 4 large onions, thinly sliced
  • 10 sheets phyllo dough, thawed
  • 140g (1 cup) blanched almonds
  • 6 large eggs
  • 1 tsp ground turmeric
  • 1 tsp ground cinnamon, plus more for dusting
  • Large pinch of saffron threads, bloomed in 2 tbsp warm water
  • 20ml (4 tsp) orange blossom water
  • 2 tbsp granulated sugar (for the almond mixture)
  • 4 tbsp melted butter (or olive oil for pareve version)
  • Salt and pepper
  • Powdered (icing) sugar for dusting

Directions

  1. Place the chicken thighs in a large pot with the sliced onions, saffron (with its soaking water), turmeric, 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon, salt, and pepper. Add enough water to barely cover the chicken. Bring to a boil, then reduce to a gentle simmer and cook for 1 hour until the chicken is falling off the bone.
  2. Remove the chicken pieces and set aside to cool. Continue cooking the onion sauce over medium heat, reducing it until it is nearly dry and the onions have broken down into a thick, jammy consistency. This concentrated onion sauce is a key layer of flavor.
  3. While the sauce reduces, shred the chicken meat, discarding the skin and bones. Set the shredded chicken aside.
  4. When the onion sauce is thick and nearly dry, reduce the heat to low. Whisk the 6 eggs in a bowl and pour them into the hot onion sauce, stirring constantly with a wooden spoon. The eggs will cook gently in the residual heat and the thick sauce, forming a creamy, custardy mixture — not scrambled eggs. This should take 3 to 4 minutes of constant stirring. Remove from heat and set aside.
  5. Toast the blanched almonds in a dry skillet over medium heat for 3 to 4 minutes, shaking frequently, until golden and fragrant. Let cool, then pulse in a food processor with the orange blossom water, sugar, and 1/2 teaspoon cinnamon until you have a coarse, aromatic mixture.
  6. Preheat the oven to 375°F. Brush a 10-inch round baking pan or oven-safe skillet with melted butter.
  7. Layer 4 sheets of phyllo in the pan, brushing each generously with melted butter and allowing the edges to overhang the sides of the pan.
  8. Spread the egg-onion custard in an even layer over the phyllo base. Top with the shredded chicken in an even layer. Spread the almond mixture over the chicken.
  9. Fold the overhanging phyllo edges over the filling. Top with 2 more sheets of phyllo, each brushed with butter, tucking the edges down the sides of the pan to seal.
  10. Bake for 20 minutes until the top is golden. Carefully invert the pastilla onto a baking sheet (or place another baking sheet on top and flip). Return to the oven and bake for 15 more minutes until both sides are deeply golden and crispy.
  11. Dust the hot pastilla generously with powdered sugar and draw decorative lines of ground cinnamon across the top. Serve immediately, cutting into wedges like a pie.
Chef's Tip

The egg layer should be a creamy custard, not scrambled eggs — keep the heat low and stir constantly. The dusting of powdered sugar and cinnamon on a savory dish is the hallmark of Moroccan pastilla and should not be skipped; it creates the signature sweet-savory contrast.
