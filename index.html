Our Mission

Every Jewish Soul Is a Letter in the Torah

"When one letter is missing, the scroll is incomplete. When one person is missing, the people are incomplete."

— Inspired by the Zohar

The Baal Shem Tov taught that the Jewish people itself is a living Sefer Torah — and every single Jew is one of its sacred letters. This platform was built so you can discover your letter, study with friends, and carry the scroll forward to the next generation.

A Letter in the Scroll is a free, non-profit labor of love — no ads, no paywalls, no subscriptions. Just Torah, community, and the belief that learning is better together.

Frequently Asked Questions

A Letter in the Scroll is a free, non-profit interactive platform for Torah study. You can form a chavruta (study partnership), explore the weekly Parsha with verse-by-verse text, learn about Jewish holidays, read daily Psalms, and grow together with friends in a private study group.
Yes, completely free — no ads, no paywalls, no premium tiers. This is a non-profit labor of love. Every feature on the platform is available to everyone at no cost. If you'd like to support the project, you can buy us a coffee, but it's never required.
A chavruta (Hebrew: חַבְרוּתָא) is a traditional Jewish method of learning sacred texts with a partner or small group. On this platform, you create a private room, invite friends with a simple link, and study the weekly Torah portion together — sharing reactions, discussions, and insights.
The platform includes all 54 weekly Torah portions (Parshat Hashavua) with verse-by-verse text, 150 Psalms (Tehillim), 20+ Jewish holiday guides with traditions and songs, daily inspirational quotes from Torah and Jewish wisdom, Shabbat prayers and zemirot, and tools for group study like reactions and bookmarks.
Not at all. All Torah portions and content include English translations alongside the Hebrew text. The platform is designed to be welcoming for learners at every level — from absolute beginners to experienced scholars.
After creating an account, you can start a new chavruta room from your dashboard. You'll get a unique invite link to share with friends. When they click it, they'll be guided to create an account and join your study group automatically.
