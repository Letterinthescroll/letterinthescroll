Overview

What Is Teacher Mode?

Teacher Mode transforms A Letter in the Scroll from a personal Torah study tool into a full classroom experience. As a teacher, you create a dedicated classroom space where your students join using a simple invite code. Everything they do — reading, commenting, reacting — stays scoped to your classroom.

Think of it like Google Classroom, but built specifically for Torah learning. You can post announcements, create assignments tied to specific parsha chapters, and see at a glance which students are engaging with the material each week.

Built for Jewish Educators

Whether you teach at a day school, yeshiva, synagogue, or run a private study group, Teacher Mode gives you the tools to guide your students through the weekly Torah portion with accountability and community. And it's completely free — no subscriptions, no trials, no ads.

Features

Everything You Get as a Teacher

Teacher Mode gives you a dedicated classroom dashboard with powerful tools designed specifically for Torah education.

Announcements

Post updates to your entire classroom. Students see announcements in the Stream tab as soon as they open the classroom.

Assignments

Create assignments with a title, description, due date, and specific parsha/chapter references so students know exactly what to study.

Student Management

See all members at a glance, approve join requests, and manage your classroom roster with up to 80 students.

Private Messaging

Send direct messages to individual students. Conversations stay private between you and the student within the classroom.

Weekly Activity Analytics

See which students commented, hearted, or highlighted verses this week. Quickly verify that students completed their assignments.

Scoped Reactions

When students study in your classroom, their hearts, highlights, and comments stay within the classroom — they won't leak to other groups.

Getting Started

Set Up Your Classroom in 3 Minutes

No complicated onboarding, no waiting for approval. You can have your first classroom running today.

  1. 1

    Create an Account

    Sign up for a free account on A Letter in the Scroll using your email or Google login. It takes about 30 seconds.

  2. 2

    Toggle Teacher Mode & Create a Classroom

    On your Dashboard, toggle on Teacher Mode (the graduation cap icon at the top). Then click Create Study Group and check the "Create as Classroom" option. Name your classroom and set your preferences.

  3. 3

    Share the Invite Code

    Every classroom gets a unique invite code. Share it with your students — they enter the code on their Dashboard to join instantly. You can also enable join-approval if you want to vet each student first.

  4. 4

    Post Your First Assignment

    Open your classroom, switch to the Stream tab, and create your first assignment. Link it to a specific parsha chapter, set a due date, and your students will see it the moment they open the classroom.

For Students

How Your Students Join

Joining a classroom is just as simple. Students don't need a special account type — any free account works.

  1. 1

    Create a Free Account

    Students sign up at aletterinthescroll.com with their email or Google account.

  2. 2

    Enter the Invite Code

    On the Dashboard, students click "Join Study Group" and enter the classroom invite code you shared with them.

  3. 3

    Start Studying

    Once joined, students can open the classroom, read the weekly parsha, comment on verses, heart meaningful passages, and complete assignments. Everything they do is visible to you in the Activity tab.

FAQ

Common Questions

Is Teacher Mode really free?

Yes, completely. A Letter in the Scroll is a non-profit project. There are no subscriptions, no premium tiers, and no ads. Teacher Mode is included at no cost.

How many students can join a classroom?

Each classroom supports up to 80 students. If you need more, you can create multiple classrooms.

Can students be in multiple classrooms?

Absolutely. Students can join as many classrooms and study groups as they like. Their activity in each classroom stays separate.

Can I moderate comments?

Yes. As the classroom leader, you can delete any comment or chat message within your classroom. There's also an optional safety filter that screens for inappropriate language.

What content can my students study?

Students have access to the full weekly Torah portion (parsha) with bilingual Hebrew-English text, classical commentary, and verse-by-verse study. They can also access Psalms, prayers, holidays, and songs.

Can I see what my students are doing?

Yes. The Activity tab in your classroom shows a weekly breakdown of each student's engagement — how many comments, hearts, and highlights they've made. You can verify at a glance who completed their assignments.

Do I need any technical skills?

Not at all. If you can send an email, you can run a classroom. The interface is designed to be intuitive for educators of all backgrounds.
