Sephardic · Edot HaMizrach

Daily Prayer Guidelines

The morning order, step by step — from the first breath of gratitude to the fifteen morning blessings, in full Hebrew, transliteration, and English.

Tailored for a

The day begins the moment you wake. Sleep, our sages teach, is a fractional taste of death — the soul ascends through the night and returns at dawn. This order restores the spirit step by step: first gratitude, then the washing that lifts the night's impurity, then the blessings that greet the day.

New to this? Read this first. Follow the steps in order, top to bottom. Each step has a blue “How & when” box telling you exactly what to do, whether to sit or stand, and whether you need others present. Almost everything here is done alone — you do not need a synagogue or a minyan (a group of ten) for the morning blessings. A minyan only matters for the communal service that comes afterward. Use the Man / Woman switch at the top — the prayers update to the correct wording for you.
1

Modeh / Modah Ani

The first words — while still in bed

The instant you wake — before rising, before touching your eyes, mouth, or any food — acknowledge the return of your soul. Because the hands are not yet washed, this prayer contains no holy Name of God, so it may be said with unwashed hands while still lying down. A man says Modeh; a woman says Modah.

How & when: The very first thing you say, lying in bed with eyes barely open, before you get up. Say it once, quietly, in any language you understand if you can't read the Hebrew. No washing, no standing, no minyan needed — just gratitude.
Modeh Ani · for a man
מוֹדֶה אֲנִי לְפָנֶיךָ מֶלֶךְ חַי וְקַיָּם, שֶׁהֶחֱזַרְתָּ בִּי נִשְׁמָתִי בְּחֶמְלָה, רַבָּה אֱמוּנָתֶךָ.
Modeh ani lefanekha, Melekh chai vekayam, shehechezarta bi nishmati bechemlah, rabbah emunatekha.
I give thanks before You, living and everlasting King, for You have returned my soul within me with compassion. Great is Your faithfulness.
Modah Ani · for a woman
מוֹדָה אֲנִי לְפָנֶיךָ מֶלֶךְ חַי וְקַיָּם, שֶׁהֶחֱזַרְתָּ בִּי נִשְׁמָתִי בְּחֶמְלָה, רַבָּה אֱמוּנָתֶךָ.
Modah ani lefanekha, Melekh chai vekayam, shehechezarta bi nishmati bechemlah, rabbah emunatekha.
I give thanks before You, living and everlasting King, for You have returned my soul within me with compassion. Great is Your faithfulness.
2

Netilat Yadayim

The morning washing of the hands
How & when: Do this as soon as you get out of bed, before you do almost anything else. You need a cup (any cup with two handles is traditional, but any cup works) and a sink. Stand at the sink; this is a private act — no minyan. The blessing comes after the pouring, while your hands are still wet and raised.

Go directly to a sink — ideally in the kitchen, never the bathroom (blessings may not be said there). Use a cup or vessel (a natla), not the faucet directly. To drive off the night's impurity, the pours must alternate between hands:

  1. Take the cup in your right hand, fill it, and pass it to your left hand.
  2. Pour over the entire right hand, up to the wrist.
  3. Pass the cup back to the right hand and pour over the entire left hand.
  4. Repeat the alternating pattern — Right, Left, Right, Left, Right, Left — three times each, six pours in all.

Recite the blessing after washing but before drying, holding your wet hands up before you. Do not let the water run back down onto your wrists.

Blessing on washing the hands
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֺתָיו, וְצִוָּנוּ עַל נְטִילַת יָדָיִם.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, asher kideshanu bemitzvotav vetzivanu al netilat yadayim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments, and commanded us concerning the washing of the hands.
Sephardic note: If you must use the bathroom first, go before washing. Afterward, wash once with the six alternating pours and recite the blessing then. Do not wash twice with two blessings — once the obligation is fulfilled, a second blessing would be said in vain (bracha l'vatala).
3

Asher Yatzar

After using the restroom & washing

Gratitude for the body's intricate workings — that the systems which must stay open remain open, and those that must stay closed remain closed.

How & when: Said every time you use the bathroom and wash your hands — including this first morning time — not only in the morning. Recited seated or standing, alone, no minyan. If you didn't need the bathroom on waking, skip it for now and say it later when you do.
Asher Yatzar · the body
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר יָצַר אֶת הָאָדָם בְּחָכְמָה, וּבָרָא בוֹ נְקָבִים נְקָבִים, חֲלוּלִים חֲלוּלִים. גָּלוּי וְיָדוּעַ לִפְנֵי כִסֵּא כְבוֹדֶךָ, שֶׁאִם יִפָּתֵחַ אֶחָד מֵהֶם, אוֹ יִסָּתֵם אֶחָד מֵהֶם, אִי אֶפְשַׁר לְהִתְקַיֵּם וְלַעֲמוֹד לְפָנֶיךָ אֲפִילּוּ שָׁעָה אֶחָת. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ, רוֹפֵא כָל בָּשָׂר וּמַפְלִיא לַעֲשׂוֹת.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, asher yatzar et ha'adam bechochmah, u'vara vo nekavim nekavim, chalulim chalulim. Galui veyadua lifnei chisei kevodekha, she'im yipatei'ach echad meihem, o yisateim echad meihem, ee efshar lehitkayeim vela'amod lefanekha afilu sha'ah achat. Baruch atah Adonai, rofei chol basar u'mafli la'asot.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who formed man in wisdom and created within him numerous orifices and cavities. It is revealed and known before the Throne of Your Glory that if but one of them were to be opened, or one of them were to be blocked, it would be impossible to exist and stand before You even for a single hour. Blessed are You, Lord, who heals all flesh and acts wondrously.
4

Elohai Neshamah

The purity of the soul

Said directly after Asher Yatzar — turning from the body to the soul, thanking God for the pure soul entrusted to us. A man says modeh; a woman says modah (the word changes automatically below to match your selection).

How & when: Once a day, in the morning, right after Asher Yatzar. Seated or standing, alone — no minyan. Many people flow straight from here into the Birkot HaShachar below without pausing.
Elohai Neshamah · the soul
אֱלֹהַי, נְשָׁמָה שֶׁנָּתַתָּ בִּי טְהוֹרָה הִיא. אַתָּה בְרָאתָהּ, אַתָּה יְצַרְתָּהּ, אַתָּה נְפַחְתָּהּ בִּי, וְאַתָּה מְשַׁמְּרָהּ בְּקִרְבִּי, וְאַתָּה עָתִיד לִטְּלָהּ מִמֶּנִּי, וּלְהַחֲזִירָהּ בִּי לֶעָתִיד לָבוֹא. כָּל זְמַן שֶׁהַנְּשָׁמָה בְקִרְבִּי, מוֹדֶהמוֹדָה אֲנִי לְפָנֶיךָ, יְיָ אֱלֹהַי וֵאלֹהֵי אֲבוֹתַי, רִבּוֹן כָּל הַמַּעֲשִׂים, אֲדוֹן כָּל הַנְּשָׁמוֹת. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ, הַמַּחֲזִיר נְשָׁמוֹת לִפְגָרִים מֵתִים.
Elohai, neshamah shenatata bi tehorah hi. Atah veratah, atah yetzartah, atah nefachtah bi, ve'atah meshamrah bekirbi, ve'atah atid litelah mimeni, ulehachazirah bi le'atid lavo. Kol z'man shehaneshamah bekirbi, modehmodah ani lefanekha, Adonai Elohai ve'lohei avotai, Ribbon kol hama'asim, Adon kol haneshamot. Baruch atah Adonai, hamachazir neshamot lifgarim metim.
My God, the soul You placed within me is pure. You created it, You formed it, You breathed it into me, You preserve it within me, and one day You will take it from me and restore it to me in the time to come. As long as the soul is within me, I give thanks before You, Lord my God and God of my ancestors, Master of all works, Lord of all souls. Blessed are You, Lord, who restores souls to lifeless bodies.
The Fifteen Morning Blessings
5

Birkot HaShachar

The morning blessings, recited in order

A continuous sequence mirroring the return of consciousness. This list follows the authentic Sephardic (Edot HaMizrach) order, which differs from the Ashkenazic one — the three “identity” blessings come near the end. Each is given here in full.

How & when: Say all fifteen standing or seated, but seated is the norm — there is no need to stand. Recite them after washing, in the order below, ideally first thing in the morning at home, or in the synagogue before the service. They do not require a minyan — you say them alone. Read each one aloud (or in a soft whisper); it is best to understand the words, so use the English alongside the Hebrew.
1 Discernment
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, הַנּוֹתֵן לַשֶּׂכְוִי בִינָה לְהַבְחִין בֵּין יוֹם וּבֵין לָיְלָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, hanotein lasechvi vinah lehavchin bein yom u'vein lailah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who gives the rooster understanding to distinguish between day and night.
2 Sight
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, פּוֹקֵחַ עִוְרִים.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, pokei'ach ivrim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who opens the eyes of the blind.
3 Freedom of movement
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, מַתִּיר אֲסוּרִים.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, matir asurim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who releases the bound.
4 Posture
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, זוֹקֵף כְּפוּפִים.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, zokef kefufim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who straightens the bent.
5 Clothing
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, מַלְבִּישׁ עֲרֻמִּים.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, malbish arumim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who clothes the naked.
6 Vitality
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, הַנּוֹתֵן לַיָּעֵף כֹּחַ.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, hanotein laya'ef koach.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who gives strength to the weary.
7 The earth
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, רוֹקַע הָאָרֶץ עַל הַמָּיִם.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, roka ha'aretz al hamayim.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who spreads the earth upon the waters.
8 Guidance
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, הַמֵּכִין מִצְעֲדֵי גָבֶר.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, hameichin mitzadei gaver.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who prepares the steps of man.
9 Provision
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁעָשָׂה לִי כָּל צָרְכִּי.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, she'asah li kol tzorki.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has provided me with all my needs.
10 Strength
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אוֹזֵר יִשְׂרָאֵל בִּגְבוּרָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, ozer Yisrael bigvurah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who girds Israel with strength.
11 Glory
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, עוֹטֵר יִשְׂרָאֵל בְּתִפְאָרָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, oter Yisrael b'tifarah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who crowns Israel with glory.
12 Spiritual identity · for a man
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא עָשַׂנִי גּוֹי.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, shelo asani goy.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has not made me a gentile.
12 Spiritual identity · for a woman
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא עָשַׂנִי גּוֹיָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, shelo asani goyah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has not made me a gentile. (feminine form: goyah)
13 Freedom · for a man
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא עָשַׂנִי עָבֶד.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, shelo asani aved.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has not made me a slave.
13 Freedom · for a woman
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא עָשַׂנִי שִׁפְחָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, shelo asani shifchah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has not made me a maidservant. (feminine form: shifchah)
14 Spiritual role · for a man
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, שֶׁלֹּא עָשַׂנִי אִשָּׁה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, shelo asani ishah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has not made me a woman.
14 Spiritual role · for a woman
בָּרוּךְ שֶׁעָשַׂנִי כִּרְצוֹנוֹ.
Baruch she'asani kirtzono.
Blessed is He who has made me according to His will.
Sephardic ruling: A woman recites this blessing without God's Name and Kingship — simply “Baruch she'asani kirtzono” — because of a doubt in its formulation, following the principle of leniency with blessings (safek brachot l'hakel).
15 Wakefulness
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, הַמַּעֲבִיר שֵׁנָה מֵעֵינָי וּתְנוּמָה מֵעַפְעַפָּי.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, hama'avir sheinah mei'einai u'tnumah mei'afapai.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who removes sleep from my eyes and slumber from my eyelids.
Blessings over the Torah
6

Birchot HaTorah

Before any words of Torah are studied

It is forbidden to speak words of Torah before these blessings. Sephardic custom says al divrei Torah (“concerning words of Torah”). Three blessings are recited together:

How & when: Once a day, in the morning, after Birkot HaShachar. Say all three in a row without interruption. Seated or standing, alone — no minyan. After these, it is customary to immediately read a short Torah verse or two (any verse) so the blessing is “used.”
First blessing
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר קִדְּשָׁנוּ בְּמִצְוֺתָיו וְצִוָּנוּ עַל דִּבְרֵי תוֹרָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, asher kideshanu bemitzvotav vetzivanu al divrei Torah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has sanctified us with His commandments and commanded us concerning words of Torah.
Second blessing · Ve'ha'arev Na
וְהַעֲרֶב נָא יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ אֶת דִּבְרֵי תוֹרָתְךָ בְּפִינוּ וּבְפִי עַמְּךָ בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, וְנִהְיֶה אֲנַחְנוּ וְצֶאֱצָאֵינוּ וְצֶאֱצָאֵי עַמְּךָ בֵּית יִשְׂרָאֵל, כֻּלָּנוּ יוֹדְעֵי שְׁמֶךָ וְלוֹמְדֵי תוֹרָתֶךָ לִשְׁמָהּ. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ, הַמְלַמֵּד תּוֹרָה לְעַמּוֹ יִשְׂרָאֵל.
Veha'arev na Adonai Eloheinu et divrei toratecha befinu uvefi amecha beit Yisrael, venihyeh anachnu vetze'etza'einu vetze'etza'ei amecha beit Yisrael, kulanu yodei shemecha velomdei toratecha lishmah. Baruch atah Adonai, hamelamed Torah le'amo Yisrael.
Please, Lord our God, make the words of Your Torah sweet in our mouths and in the mouths of Your people the House of Israel, that we and our descendants and the descendants of Your people the House of Israel may all know Your Name and study Your Torah for its own sake. Blessed are You, Lord, who teaches Torah to His people Israel.
Third blessing · Asher Bachar Banu
בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ אֱלֹהֵינוּ מֶלֶךְ הָעוֹלָם, אֲשֶׁר בָּחַר בָּנוּ מִכָּל הָעַמִּים וְנָתַן לָנוּ אֶת תּוֹרָתוֹ. בָּרוּךְ אַתָּה יְיָ, נוֹתֵן הַתּוֹרָה.
Baruch atah Adonai, Eloheinu Melech ha'olam, asher bachar banu mikol ha'amim venatan lanu et torato. Baruch atah Adonai, notein haTorah.
Blessed are You, Lord our God, King of the universe, who has chosen us from among all the peoples and given us His Torah. Blessed are You, Lord, who gives the Torah.
For a man · before Shacharit
7

Tallit & Tefillin

Worn by men before the morning Amidah

These are positive, time-bound commandments — an explicit obligation for men, from which women are exempt. Inspect the fringes of the Tallit, then wrap it around your head and shoulders while reciting:

How & when: For weekday mornings only (Tefillin is not worn on Shabbat or festivals). Put on the Tallit first, then the Tefillin, standing, just before the morning service. You don't need a minyan to wear them, but the morning Amidah is best said with one. If you're new to this, ask someone to show you how to wind the straps the first time.

One Prayer a Day

The Sephardic obligation for women

Following the Rambam and codified by Rabbi Ovadia Yosef, a woman is obligated to pray once daily, not bound to the three fixed services. The advice is to keep one consistent routine — usually Shacharit — so it is never forgotten. If she prays in the morning, her minimal requirement is:

  • Birkot HaShachar — the morning blessings (above).
  • Birchot HaTorah — the Torah blessings (above).
  • The first verse of the Shema — declaring God's oneness.
  • The Amidah — the silent standing prayer, the core of prayer.
A woman recites these prayers and blessings even while menstruating (niddah). Mainstream Sephardic halacha holds that ritual impurity does not prevent a woman from praying, entering a synagogue, or studying Torah.

Shema Yisrael

The declaration of God's oneness — first paragraph
How & when: Said within the morning prayers (and again at night). When you say the first line (“Shema Yisrael…”), the custom is to cover your eyes with your right hand and concentrate on the meaning — God is One. The second line is said in an undertone (whispered). No minyan required, though the full Shema is part of the communal service.
